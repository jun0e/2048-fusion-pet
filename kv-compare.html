2048 Siege: key visual demo

The recommended art direction running in the real game, with a pixel-art map beside it for comparison. Same seed, same board, same waves: only the map art differs. In both, the namecard portraits are the pet's own 2D sticker on a flat plate. Tap a lit namecard to cast its skill.

Map tiles, 16 to 128

The number owns the top of the square; the baby peeks over the bottom edge and grows one motif piece per merge. Eggs (2, 4, 8) crack open.

Roaming fusions, 256+

The fusion hops out and roams the field; its tile stays as a den with a split plate and both glyphs. Body from parent A, crest and tail from parent B.

Namecards

2D sticker portraits: dimmed while charging, full colour with a ring and shine when ready, a cut-in when cast. A pure form wears gold: its portrait has a halo, on a gold plate.

Stand-in art

Every creature here is drawn in code from the style brief. When Gemini billing is on, generated art replaces it through the same layers.
